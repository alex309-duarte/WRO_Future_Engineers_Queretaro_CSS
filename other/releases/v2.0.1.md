## v2.0.1 — International Final version (design v7), updated assembly instructions

**Date:** 2026-11-16

**Current version** of the documentation and code of ***"The Maker"***, design v7, for the **WRO 2026 Future Engineers International Final** (Puerto Rico, December 2026). It replaces v2.0.0 with new assembly instructions photographed on the design v7 robot, plus corrections. Design v6, the vehicle of the Mexico National Final (2nd place), stays available as release `v1.0.0`.

### Changes since v2.0.0
- **New assembly instructions** (`other/Assembly_photos/Assembly_Process_EN.md`): 37 steps in 11 phases, each with a photo of the design v7 build. Includes a tools and materials table, battery safety rules (spot-welded nickel strips, no soldering iron on the cells, short circuits, solder fumes) and a parts list with every screw, standoff, heat-set insert and small LEGO part. The README §10 table links each phase.
- **Steering servo corrected to the Hiwonder HX-12H** in the README, the journal, the diagrams, the source comments and the component photo; peak-current calculation redone with the HX-12H stall current.
- **Battery layout:** two cells end to end in four generic interlocking 18650 spacer brackets and the third cell in a printed holder, all at the same height, so the Hiwonder board sits lower and no longer blocks the LiDAR (small modification of design v7; failure log F12).
- **Print files:** 15 STL files (19 pieces): new `battery_holder.STL`, `motor_gear_20t.STL` and `hiwonder_support.STL` (controller board plate); new parts preview.
- **Bill of Materials:** HX-12H price; standoff, screw and heat-set insert kits and the small LEGO parts added; new total $26,139.37 MXN.
- **Engineering Journal** updated to match (servo, battery layout, print files, budget, logbook, release table).
