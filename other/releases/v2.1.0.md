## v2.1.0 — International Final version (design v7)

**Date:** 2026-11-22

**Current version** of the documentation and code of ***"The Maker"***, design v7, for the **WRO 2026 Future Engineers International Final** (Puerto Rico, December 2026). Design v6, the vehicle of the Mexico National Final (2nd place), stays available as release `v1.0.0`.

### New in v2.1.0: a digital twin of the Obstacle Challenge
- **The robot's own program in a simulator.** In `simulation/`, a CoppeliaSim 4.10 scene runs the Obstacle Challenge program of this repository, compiled without changes, against a simulated "The Maker": the Hiwonder board, the RPLidar S2L, the camera and the detector are simulated, the field follows the rules and the car comes from the CAD model. A referee in the scene records the laps, the side on which every sign is passed, signs touched, wall contacts and the time. Instructions to build and run it are in `simulation/README.md`.
- **What it found.** A single sign right after a corner leaves the robot about 300 mm of road, and in Layout A clockwise the red sign of the parking section is hit on lap 2. When the computer falls behind the camera rate, the mission can decide on camera images more than a second old.
- **A proposed change to the mission** (`simulation/proposed/`): reverse, read the colour again and change lane on a LiDAR measurement before passing the sign; plus a 200 ms hold before the colour of a corner sign is read. In the twin, Layout A clockwise: 3 laps, 15 of 15 signs on the correct side, 1:54.6. **Tested only in the twin; not applied to `src/` and not tested on the robot.** Layouts B to E, Layout A counter-clockwise and the final parking are not solved in the twin yet.
- **Surprise rule prototype** (in the twin only): the height of a sign measured from its detection box and the camera geometry; the modified sign passed on the side of its original colour in 4 of 4 runs.
- **README:** new section 6.11 and a new step in the testing workflow (6.9); the surprise-rule section (6.10) shows the prototype; the twin video is at the top and in section 11; known limitations (7.6) and repository structure (8.2) updated. Figures renumbered 1 to 19 in reading order.
