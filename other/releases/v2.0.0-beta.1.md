## v2.0.0-beta.1 — International Final documentation, feature-complete (pre-release)

**Date:** 2026-11-09

Feature-complete documentation of design v7 for the **WRO 2026 Future Engineers International Final** (Puerto Rico, December 2026). Everything for the final submission is in place except the driving-video links.

### Highlights
- **Software sections re-traced from the v7 code:** Open Challenge flowchart and thresholds (12 corners, 70–80° corner turns, single final stop at 1500 mm), Obstacle Challenge changes and thresholds (corner approach 1000 mm), and the v7 steering PD chain with the gains re-tuned on the track.
- **Design decisions completed:** why the JGB37 motor, why the Hiwonder board replaced the SPIKE Hub, why the model was retrained (missed pillars, false detections outside the track and in empty corners, poor lighting), and the new rear differential (same 20:28 ratio).
- **Power calculations from the Panasonic NCR18650B datasheet:** average and peak pack current, voltage sag, lowest board input voltage, energy per round and runtime.
- **Hiwonder ROS Robot Controller specification table**, hardware kill switch (board power switch) and an updated risk register.
- **CAD views of design v7** (side, front, rear, top, isometric); LiDAR scanning plane at about 75 mm above the ground.
- **Datasheets kept in the repository** (`other/datasheets/`) together with the WRO documentation rubric and playfield (`other/`), with links to the original sources.
- **English documentation:** `HIWONDER.md`, `ORIGEN.md` and the Obstacle Challenge `README.md` translated; `SERVICES_README.md` rewritten for the v7 programs.
- **Engineering Journal, international edition** updated with all of the above, plus a datasheets and references section.
