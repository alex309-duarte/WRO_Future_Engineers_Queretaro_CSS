## v2.0.0-alpha.2 — International Final documentation snapshot (pre-release)

**Date:** 2026-11-09

Second development snapshot of design v7, the vehicle for the **WRO 2026 Future Engineers International Final** (Puerto Rico, December 2026). Documentation in progress; not the final submission.

### Highlights
- **Engineering Journal, international edition** (`Engineering_Journal_CSS_WRO2026_International.pdf`). The national edition remains in release `v1.0.0`.
- **README reorganised by the five WRO 2026 documentation criteria**, with decision cards (what / why / when / how / what for / alternatives) for every change since the national final.
- **Design v7 hardware documented:** Hiwonder ROS Robot Controller, JGB37-520 30:1 encoder motor, HX-35H bus servo, rear differential, 3S NCR18650B battery with BMS, RPLidar S2L, Hailo-8.
- **Wiring diagram** for design v7 (`schemes/hardware/wiringSchematics_v7.png`).
- **SolidWorks assembly of design v7** (`models/cad/v7_assembly/`).
- **Test results** from the 2026-11-08 session (`other/testing/WRO2026_v7_test_log.xlsx`): component tests, 10 Open Challenge and 10 Obstacle Challenge runs.
- **Vehicle photos** of design v7 (`v-photos/*_v7.JPG`), isometric view and national-final team photos.
- Updated bill of materials with current prices.
- Build artefacts and compiled binaries removed from version control.
