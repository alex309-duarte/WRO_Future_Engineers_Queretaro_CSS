## v2.2.0 — International Final version (design v7)

**Date:** 2026-11-23

**Current version** of the documentation and code of ***"The Maker"***, design v7, for the **WRO 2026 Future Engineers International Final** (Puerto Rico, December 2026). Design v6, the vehicle of the Mexico National Final (2nd place), stays available as release `v1.0.0`.

### New in v2.2.0: the Obstacle Challenge of design v7, seen three ways
- **New Obstacle Challenge video.** Design v7 on our practice field (22 November 2026), filmed with an external camera; the robot's camera, with the detections of its model, and its LiDAR scan were recorded at the same time and are shown above the field, synchronised. A stopwatch and a lap counter run with it: laps of 26.4, 24.6 and 25.4 s and the parking in 20.6 s, **1:36.97** in total. New preview at the top of the README, in section 11 and in `video/video.md`. Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mQtrA2Nts34
- **Overview video block** at the very top of the README, above the driving videos, with a quick link, a block in section 11 and one in `video/video.md`: an introduction to the project and a guided tour of the repository.
- **Four GIFs of the same 10 s of that run:** the three views side by side (section 6.3), the external view (section 2), the camera with the detections (section 6.4) and the LiDAR window (section 6.5). They are in `other/additionalMedia/Others/`.
- **Run log:** the filmed run is added to section 6.9.2.
- **Design v7 re-measured** after the battery and the controller board were lowered: **1.013 kg** and **245 × 157.7 × 180 mm**. The specifications, the torque and speed calculation, the constraints chart and the Engineering Journal use the new values.
- **Text corrections** in the descriptions of the parking, the LiDAR mounting and the vision model.
- **Engineering Journal:** the filmed run, the re-measured vehicle, the logbook and the list of releases.
