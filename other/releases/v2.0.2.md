## v2.0.2 — International Final version (design v7)

**Date:** 2026-11-21

**Current version** of the documentation and code of ***"The Maker"***, design v7, for the **WRO 2026 Future Engineers International Final** (Puerto Rico, December 2026). Design v6, the vehicle of the Mexico National Final (2nd place), stays available as release `v1.0.0`.

### Changes since v2.0.1
- **README reorganised for a faster review.** The driving videos are at the top; a new table, "Where each rubric item is answered", links every item of the documentation rubric to its section; "Build this robot yourself" lists the five steps to reproduce the vehicle.
- **Shorter page, nothing removed.** Supporting detail (constants, long tables and calculations, specification tables) is folded into blocks marked "Click to open"; photos, diagrams, decision cards and results stay visible. The decision cards are highlighted in purple boxes.
- **Pages of their own:** the full setup and execution instructions and the design v6 (national) documentation are now in `other/docs/`, with a quick start and links in the README. `SERVICES_README.md` moved to `src/`.
- **New figures:** power budget, LiDAR scanning plane, LiDAR accuracy, run logs, constraints, cost by category, the parking manoeuvre seen from above, and clickable previews of the linked documents. The obstacle layout drawings now place the parking lot as the rules define it.
- **Design v6 next to design v7:** both vehicles side by side, with the same six views, in "What changed from design v6 to design v7".
- **Surprise rule (modified traffic sign):** the rule is in the repository (`other/WRO-2026-FE_SurpriseRule.pdf`) and README §6.10 describes our plan for it, which is still in development.
- **New photos of the vehicle** after the battery layout change (top, front, back, left and right views, and the rotating view).
- **Team section:** members side by side; the biographies are folded.
- **Engineering Journal:** diagrams corrected to the HX-12H servo, new photos, a section on the surprise rule and new logbook entries.
